Grades 6 – 8

Bridging Concrete Arithmetic to Abstract Thinking

Middle school is a pivotal transition period. Math shifts from arithmetic rules to abstract reasoning. Mr. Taye provides calm, step-by-step guidance to ensure students don't fall behind during this crucial stage.

Key Academic Concepts Covered

  • Pre-Algebra Readiness: Variables, expressions, and solving simple one-step and multi-step equations.
  • Ratios, Rates, & Percentages: Real-world mathematical applications and unit rate calculations.
  • Introductory Geometry: Understanding perimeter, area, volume, coordinate planes, and angles.
  • Study & Test Preparation: Techniques for reviewing homework efficiently and tackling school exams calmly.
