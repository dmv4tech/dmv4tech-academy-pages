Building Joy and Curiosity in Early Mathematics

Early experiences with math shape a student’s lifelong confidence. With over 30 years of patient teaching, Mr. Taye helps young learners master essential foundational concepts without feeling overwhelmed or rushed.

Focus Areas for Early Learners

  • Number Sense & Basic Operations: Mastering addition, subtraction, multiplication tables, and division with ease.
  • Fractions & Decimals: Visualizing parts of a whole through step-by-step visual breakdowns.
  • Word Problems: Turning wordy problem statements into simple, clear mathematical steps.
  • Building Positive Habits: Developing neat handwriting, organized work habits, and stress-free study routines.
